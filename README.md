# cupinudous

stuck learning something technical? cupinudous turns research-backed learning techniques into simple, practical steps you can try right now, for math, computer science and similar subjects.

pick what you're struggling with from a menu (or search for it) and get a short plan of steps, each one linked to the research behind it.

## why the name

named after cup noodles, the fuel of many late-night coding sessions (chicken flavor, preferably). easy to say in english and portuguese.

## status

🫟 early setup.

## research content

research content belongs to its authors: cupinudous stores metadata, links and original summaries only, and credits every source.
